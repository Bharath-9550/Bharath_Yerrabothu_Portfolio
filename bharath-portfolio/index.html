Personal Project / Evolving Concept

Domain Learning
Platform.

Domain Learning Platform is a personal project I am designing to help new graduates and early-career technology professionals understand how real-world business domains and enterprise IT systems operate. Instead of teaching technologies in isolation, the platform will connect business processes, software applications, APIs, databases, data pipelines, engineering roles, and production scenarios within actual industry domains.

This concept is currently in development. The scope, architecture, and learning experience will evolve as the first domain prototype is built.
